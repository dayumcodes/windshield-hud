Windshield HUD
Free · no account · no tracking

Your phone is now a HUD.

Lay it on the dashboard. Speed, speed limits, turn-by-turn directions and heading glow in the windshield, right where you're already looking.

Uses GPS for speed. Best at night. Set it up before you drive.

Safety

This is a driving aid, not a replacement for your attention. Set it up before you drive, keep your eyes on the road, and follow local laws about phones in cars. Speed limits come from OpenStreetMap and can be missing or wrong - road signs win.

Privacy: everything runs on your phone. Your position is only sent to OpenStreetMap services to read speed limits and plan routes. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
